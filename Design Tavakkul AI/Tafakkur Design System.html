Tafakkur's own design language — calm, warm and reverent. Rooted in Apple's Human Interface Guidelines, but with a soul of its own: the quiet light of dawn, a single living accent, and space to breathe. Built to be loved daily.

01 — PRINCIPLES

What Fajr feels like

A self-development companion is opened in quiet moments — early mornings, between prayers, last thing at night. The system is built for that: nothing shouts, everything settles.

Calm over loud

One living accent, generous space, soft depth. We express importance through tone and light — never with noise, never with a rainbow of colors.

Reverent, not preachy

Spiritual moments get warmth and a serif voice — gold light, a breathing orb, room to reflect. Educational in tone, gentle, never dogmatic.

Intelligence, felt softly

Murabbiy, the AI, appears as a teal-to-violet glow and a breathing orb — present and personal, but private and quiet. On-device by principle.

02 — COLOR

A single light, a sacred glow

Barakah teal is the one hero accent — actions, focus, progress, the AI. Sacred gold is reserved for spiritual moments: prayer, reflection, gratitude. Everything else is warm midnight neutral.

System sans, with a soul of serif

05 — COMPONENTS

Soft, tactile, HIG-native

Inset grouped cards with a whisper of depth, pill tags, segmented controls, progress, and rings. Every control answers touch with a gentle press. Corner radii are generous — 13px controls, 16–18px cards.

06 — MOTION & ICONOGRAPHY

Quiet, breathing, tactile

Motion is gentle and purposeful — a soft cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1) ease, a slow 4.8s breath on the AI orb, and a 0.94 press-scale on every control. No bounces, no spring overshoot, nothing that hurries the user.

Press feedback

Every button and card dips slightly under touch — instantly felt, never jarring.

The breath

A 4.8s ease-in-out pulse signals the living AI and reflective focus states.

Iconography

Stroke icons at 1.6–1.8px, rounded joints, drawn warm. Outline only — never filled, never emoji in chrome.
