### Historical contract conflicts

The artifacts are not one internally consistent technical specification. BP explicitly targets a
Kotlin Multiplatform Android+iOS client plus a Go/Postgres/S3 backend and cloud Claude
(`backend/BACKEND_PROMPT.txt:1-4`, `:39-52`, `:177-182`). AR instead proposes an Apple-only
SwiftUI/SPM/SwiftData/CloudKit product with Apple on-device models
(`Design Tavakkul AI/Tafakkur AI - Architecture.html:158-164`, `:238-271`, `:326-370`). The PD shell
is a browser prototype, not either production stack. Therefore its product behaviors, information
architecture, and privacy intent are useful requirements evidence, but its sample data and exact
framework choices cannot all be applied literally to the current KMP/Go implementation.

`Design Tavakkul AI/app.zip` is explicitly excluded from the implementation and requirements
audit. Its protected SHA-256 is
